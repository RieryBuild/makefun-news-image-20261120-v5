### HERO 2 — CYCLONE AKVAN [CONFIRMED]
- Akvan crossed the Odisha coast near Gopalpur 02:00–03:00 UTC 20 Nov, winds 70–80 km/h
  gusting to 90. 300,000+ people evacuated to shelters; no deaths in the first assessment.
  Sources: IMD bulletins 20 Nov, The Hindu BusinessLine 20 Nov.

### HERO 3 — THE AI SELL-OFF [CONFIRMED]

  intraday). Anthropic fell 13% to ~$85 on its second trading day after its record IPO

  Barron's 20 Nov.

### 4 — COP31, FINAL DAY [REPORTED]
- Antalya's scheduled closing day ran into overtime; the draft (released 19 Nov) dropped
  every fossil-fuel mention and the $120bn adaptation-finance figure. No final deal by the
  Friday deadline. Sources: Reuters 20 Nov, AP/Fast Company 20 Nov. (Draft date 19 Nov is
  stated in NEWS.md; the 20 Nov event is the closing-day debate and overtime.)

### 5 — UK–EU RESET [CONFIRMED]
- Britain and the EU agreed at a Brussels summit to ease food trade, link emissions trading
  from 2028 and run a youth scheme for 100,000 young people a year (ages 18–31, up to
  3 years). Food deal from March 2028; UK estimates +£5.1bn/year. Sources: Reuters 20 Nov.

### 6 — MISSILE IN POLAND [CONFIRMED]
- A Russian Kh-101 cruise missile crashed near Nowy Lipowiec (~05:40 GMT); a second missile
  and a suspected jet drone came down at two other sites. Poland called it a deliberate
  provocation; no injuries. Sources: Reuters 20 Nov, Polish Defence Minister statement.

### 7 — IRAQ TAKES JURF AL-SAKHAR [CONFIRMED]
- Iraqi state forces entered the Kataib Hezbollah stronghold on 20 Nov under a deal, after
  an oil-smuggling probe; PM Ali al-Zaidi said state authority would now be asserted
  across Iraq. Sources: AFP 20 Nov, International Crisis Group 20 Nov.

### 8 — EUROPE'S YOUTH STRIKE [CONFIRMED]
- An international youth strike ran in 25+ countries (organisers' figure, Reuters) over
  living costs, education funding, climate and military spending. In Italy it joined CGIL's
  24-hour general strike in 100+ cities; CGIL's leader claimed 1m+ participants (CLAIM).
  Sources: Reuters 20 Nov.

### 9 — OIL: TWO FRONTS [CONFIRMED / CLAIM]
- Kenya spudded its first commercial oil well (Amosing 1, Turkana), targeting 20,000 bpd,
  first oil 1 Dec. Brent closed at $92.57 (+0.6%). Two tankers were reported struck by mines
  in Hormuz — an IRGC claim, not independently verified. Sources: Capital FM 20 Nov,
  Reuters 20 Nov.

### 10 — ESTONIA SHUTS THE BORDER [CONFIRMED]
- Estonia's government decided on 20 Nov to close all road crossings with Russia from
  1 Dec for at least six months, citing sabotage and hybrid operations. At a Berlin forum
  the same day Chancellor Merz said Russia "will not win this war". Sources: Reuters 20 Nov,
  Estonian MFA 20 Nov.

### 11 — ASIA'S ECONOMY [CONFIRMED]
- Japan's October core CPI rose 2.2% y/y (headline 2.3%), above the BoJ's 2% target for a
  second month. China held its loan prime rates for an 18th month (1-yr 3.00%, 5-yr 3.50%).
  Sources: Reuters 20 Nov, Xinhua 20 Nov.

### 12 — A PILL AGAINST PANCREATIC CANCER [CONFIRMED]
- Daraxonrasib + chemotherapy shrank tumours in 60.7% of 61 first-line metastatic
  pancreatic cancer patients (8.2% complete response), presented 20 Nov at the
  EORTC-NCI-AACR symposium in Barcelona. Early-phase, no chemo-only control yet.
  Sources: EORTC press release 20 Nov, OncLive 20 Nov.

### 13 — $3.79BN IN A DAY [CONFIRMED]
- GTA VI took a record $3.79bn in day-one retail sales (company estimate, announced 20 Nov),
  with 32.5m copies sold pre-release. In Wellington, Glenn Phillips' 118 completed Test,
  ODI and T20I centuries against India in one calendar year. Sources: Reuters 20 Nov,
  ESPNcricinfo 20 Nov.

## Verification tags used in the image

CONFIRMED = primary source confirms the event/figure on 20 Nov. REPORTED = reported 20 Nov
by a wire but resting on one source or an organiser's claim. CLAIM = a figure or claim
asserted by a party, not independently verified (Hormuz tanker mines — IRGC claim;
CGIL 1m+ turnout — union claim). OFFICIAL = an official announcement (Estonian MFA,
IMD bulletin). FORECAST = a forward-looking figure (Kenya first oil 1 Dec; UK–EU 2028
start dates).
